Privacy Policy

Lotte Real Estate (hereinafter "Company") establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy to protect users' personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of Korea and to handle related grievances promptly and smoothly.

At a glance

  • Only a phone number is required for an online inquiry. Name and inquiry details are optional.
  • Inquiry records are retained for 1 year from collection and then deleted.
  • Search queries, phone numbers and inquiry text are not sent in analytics events.
  • Public Zigbang inquiry activity keeps transaction type, listing number and “received” status visible for the latest year. The latest 24 hours use a one-hour window; older records show the receipt date only. Prices, customer names, contact details and inquiry text are excluded.
  • We currently do not operate customer accounts, marketing subscriptions or server-synced saved areas.

1. Personal information we process

Information is collected through the website inquiry form and guided inquiry assistant. If you do not consent, you can contact us by phone instead.

The legal basis is the data subject's consent under Article 15(1)1 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. We do not use inquiry data for advertising or AI model training.

Zigbang inquiry emails are processed only to confirm and respond to consultations. The owner's Kakao self-chat alert contains the email title, strictly formatted public listing data (listing number, coarse location, floor, room layout, transaction type, price, and the Zigbang inquiry access link); it excludes customer names, phone numbers, email addresses and inquiry text. The access link is sent only as an owner-only button after the authenticated Zigbang email's official domain, path and token format pass strict validation. The button uses a LotteReal intermediary page that does not store or analyze the link value and immediately continues to the official Zigbang inquiry screen.

The public inquiry activity keeps only transaction type (jeonse, monthly rent or sale), the Zigbang listing number and “received” status visible for the latest year. Activity from the latest 24 hours uses a one-hour bucket derived from Gmail receipt time; older activity shows the receipt date only. Prices, customer identifiers and detailed inquiry text are neither stored in nor exposed through this public activity surface.

2. Visit analytics and browser storage

We use Google Analytics 4 to improve the website. Page paths, browser and device information, approximate area, visit and button interaction data, and an analytics cookie identifier may be processed.

  • URL query strings are removed from both page-location and referrer values before they are sent.
  • Google Signals and ad-personalization signals are disabled.
  • Analytics cookie expiry is limited to 90 days.
  • Analytics starts only after a separate choice to allow it, and Do Not Track is respected.
  • Search queries, phone numbers, names and inquiry text are excluded from analytics events.

Report identifiers used to prevent duplicate view counts and language choice are held in sessionStorage and removed when the browser session ends. Your analytics choice is stored in this browser's localStorage.

4. Third-party disclosure, deletion and security

We do not disclose inquiry data to third parties unless required by law or separately agreed by the data subject.

Electronic records are deleted using methods designed to prevent recovery when retention ends or the purpose is achieved. Inquiry data is protected by access controls, database policies that block public reads, and encryption in transit.
