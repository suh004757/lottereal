Seoul Real Estate Guide

How to narrow Seoul property options before you visit

If you are searching for a Seoul apartment, officetel, office, or retail space from overseas or from another city, start with area, budget, timing, and property type. We help turn broad searches into a practical shortlist.

Best fit searches

01 Area

Choose the area before the building

Songpa, Jamsil, Seokchon, and Gangnam can feel close on a map but differ by commute, schools, building type, rent level, and retail foot traffic. Tell us the daily route first.

02 Budget

Separate deposit, monthly rent, and management fees

Korean lease terms often combine deposit, monthly rent, maintenance fees, and move-in timing. A clear budget range helps us avoid options that look attractive but do not fit the total cost.

03 Type

Apartment, officetel, office, or retail space?

A family apartment, one-person officetel, corporate office, and street-level retail unit are filtered differently. We shortlist by use case, not only by price.

Before contacting us

Prepare four simple details

Preferred area, property type, budget range, and move-in or opening timeline are enough to start. We can then suggest which listings are worth seeing first.
