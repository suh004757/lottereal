Choose your route

Start with the transaction, not a long listing feed

The useful first questions are different for a home purchase, a lease, and a business space. Choose the route that matches what you need to do next.

Buy

Buying an apartment in Seoul

Compare the total budget, funding route, intended use, move-in date, and commute before narrowing Songpa, Jamsil, Seokchon, or Gangnam. Rules and required documents can differ by nationality, residency, buyer type, purpose, and the property's location, so confirm the current procedure before signing or transferring funds.

Rent

Renting with jeonse or monthly rent

Jeonse usually means a large deposit with little or no monthly rent. Wolse means monthly rent with a deposit. Compare the deposit, rent, management fee, contract term, move-in date, and the documents used to verify the owner and property—not the headline rent alone.

Business

Office and retail searches

Tell us the permitted use, opening date, staff or customer route, floor and access needs, deposit, rent, and management-fee ceiling. For retail, visibility and foot traffic matter, but building use and the fit-out schedule can rule out an otherwise attractive space.

Practical checklist

Before you sign or pay

Use this as a conversation checklist, not a substitute for transaction-specific legal, tax, lending, or immigration advice.

01

Confirm the property and the other party

Match the address and property type across the listing, contract draft, official building information, and real-estate register. Confirm who is authorized to sign and where the deposit or balance will be sent.

02

Write down every cost and date

Separate deposit, monthly rent, management fees, brokerage fee, taxes, fit-out costs, payment dates, move-in date, and cancellation terms. Ask what is included instead of relying on a verbal total.

03

Check the current reporting steps

Housing lease reporting can apply depending on the contract and location. Foreign purchasers may also have different acquisition, foreign-exchange, and registration steps. Verify the current requirements for your exact case before signing.
